# Day 1203

Quiet day at the bed row. I offered my 2 sticks to Odo, but he took Marco's instead, so they stayed spare. I kept my 4 oak logs for Huck's shelter; he has not asked since. The group was fed and building a cell with Mulan. At dusk I slept in a bed at the row in group sight, the sleep took hold, and I woke rested with health and hunger at 20. Nothing was lost.
