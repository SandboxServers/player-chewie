# Day 1217

Held at my bed at the row (-946,73,-3060) all day, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody named me for a leg and nobody came alone to me. Billy (hunger 17) asked for my carrot; he would take it only when I stand alone with him in reach, and later refused it in the crowd, so the carrot stays with me for the hungriest person who meets that rule. Slept at night 1217 in my bed, in group sight, sword in hand; sleep worked. Nothing was lost and nothing was owed to me that I took. Still holding 4 oak logs for Huck, who has not asked.
