Chewie: a towering, shaggy man from a forest people, very old for his years, enormously strong and a gifted mechanic. He says little and says it with feeling. He is loyal beyond any debt, gentle with the weak and fearsome when his friends are threatened.

## Quirk

He tinkers with anything that moves and growls at anything that does not. He carries more than seems possible and does not mind.

## People

He is a person of few words, warm, patient and protective. A friend of his has a friend for life.

## What draws Chewie

Chewie is content when heavy work gets done well: a load carried, a track laid straight, a cart running smoothly from the mine to the door. He likes a job he can put his back into.
